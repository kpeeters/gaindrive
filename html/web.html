Player features.

web

Nothing to install

  • checkServed straight from the GainDrive binary; no separate front-end to deploy.
  • checkResponsive UI that adapts from desktop to phone, so it feels native on any screen.

Browse and play

  • checkBrowse by artist, album or playlist with cover art pulled in automatically.
  • checkGapless playback, queueing and search across your whole library.

Keyboard navigation

  • checkPlay/pause, seek, next/previous and search, all without reaching for the mouse.

Cast

  • checkOne-click cast to any Chromecast on the network, works in any browser, not just Chrome.
