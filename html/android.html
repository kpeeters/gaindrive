GainDrive: Android

Connects to your GainDrive server (or any other Subsonic-compatible one) and plays your audio and video collection on your phone.

What's inside

App features.

smartphone

Modern Android app

  • checkNative Android application, not a web view.
  • checkWritten in Kotlin using modern Android components like Jetpack Compose, Material3, Room3 and MediaPlayer3.
  • checkReceives native audio or a transcoded format (default Opus 160) from the server.
dns

Multi-server

  • checkTalks to any number of audio or video servers supporting the OpenSubsonic API (including GainDrive Server).
  • checkBrowse multiple servers in a unified way, e.g. show your own server and the Bandcamp server with merged artist and album lists.
download_for_offline

Cache for off-line

  • checkKeep albums and playlists on the device and play them without a connection to the server.
  • checkAutomatically cache played tracks, with configurable cache size. With option to pin albums/tracks manually so they never get removed automatically.
cast

Supports casting

  • checkUse your phone to control audio or video playing on any Chromecast device.
  • checkUses its own Chromecast implementation, so works on de-Googled phones.
  • checkUses phone as bridge if your server is behind a VPN but the player is not (e.g. play on the hotel TV which cannot reach your server directly).
